# Reference documents

Source material for `JOBS_ATA.md`. Godot ignores this folder (`.gdignore`), so nothing here ships in the game.

## ac43-13-1b/

FAA Advisory Circular 43.13-1B CHG 1, "Acceptable Methods, Techniques, and Practices: Aircraft Inspection and Repair" (1998, change 1 in 2001). A US government work in the public domain.

| File | Pages | Chapters |
|---|---|---|
| `part4_ch6-8` | 6-31 to 8-21 | End of 6 (corrosion), 7 (hardware, safetying, control cables), start of 8 (engines, fuel) |
| `part5_ch8-11` | 8-22 to 11-10 | Rest of 8 (exhaust, propellers), 9 (landing gear, hydraulics, emergency equipment), 10 (weight and balance), start of 11 (electrical, batteries) |
| `part6_ch11` | 11-11 to 11-109 | 11 (wiring) |
| `part7_ch11-13` | 11-110 to end | End of 11, 12 (avionics, pitot-static), 13, glossary |

Each PDF has a `.txt` beside it: the same pages as plain text with the table layout kept, for searching. Parts 1 to 3 (Chapters 1 to 5 and most of 6) are not here yet.

## Not in the repo

Piper J3C-65 Cub Special owner's manual (Piper Aircraft Corporation, May 15, 1946, form SP 46-8). Its values are written into `JOBS_ATA.md` with page numbers; the scan is kept out because its copyright status is not confirmed.
