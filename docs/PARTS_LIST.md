# C85-12 parts list

Part numbers marked **yes** under *From the book* were read from Continental's C75/C85/C90/O-200 Illustrated Parts Catalog (Form X-30017A, 1979) or the FAA type certificate data sheet E-233. `AR-` numbers and the Hollis/Kestrel numbers are the game's own, used where the 1979 scan was unreadable or the part is a Piper or shop item. Prices are game prices.
