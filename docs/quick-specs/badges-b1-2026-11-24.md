## Why

Thad: the badges were cheap: each earned once, for one clean moment, mostly in the first jobs. He
chose all eight of the proposal.

## What

| Badge | Standard |
|---|---|
| Nothing Gets Past You | 5 deliveries in a row with all 8 spots checked and no comeback |
| Calibrated Hand | 10 jobs in a row with every torqued fastener in the band (jobs without torque work don't count either way) |
| Mag Whisperer | 10 run-ups in a row with both mags called right, 2 of them bad mags |
| Troubleshooter | 5 trails followed with no wrong call, one of them a look-alike |
| Steady Hands | 10 ferry flights in a row: first-try start, no ground loop; a forced landing or crash breaks it |
| Clean Sheet | 5 jobs in a row with nothing extra on the ticket |
| Barn Find | Deliver a plane that sat 10 years or more, with no comeback |
| The Hard Call | Ground a plane for a reason that grounds it |

- Look-alikes: battery or starter, plugs or magneto, and faults that share a symptom (the oil ones).
- The board shows each run's progress ("3 of 5"); the cockpit card says "Toward Steady Hands: 2 of
  10"; the task list offers what a delivery can move.
- IA needs 4 of the 8 badges (was 6). The runs and thresholds are game rules for the playtest.
- Saves: version 5 drops the old badges and starts the runs at zero.

## Acceptance

to its run (earned), with the extra conditions holding back until met; after the real delivery
the runs moved as they should and nothing was earned early; the real grounding earns The Hard
Call; an old save migrates. Ten runs, two Hard, and the screenshot run pass.
