# No days counted, no job timed (D2)

**Status**: built, iteration 16
**Date**: 2026-11-23
**Code**: `main.HANGAR_FEE`, `WRONG_GUESS`, `_wrong_guess`, `_work_step`, `_migrate` (version 3);
`ferry.call_mag`; `work.perform`; the ticket's fee and guesses lines

## Why

Thad: "i dont want days to be counted or how long a job takes". The six replacements are his choice.

## What

- No `day`, no `_advance`, no deadlines. Nothing on screen counts days or hours.
- Rent: a flat hangar fee per job ($60), charged when the job closes (delivered, grounded or
  crashed); on the ticket as "Hangar fee"; the map's estimate subtracts it.
- Calls never expire.
- A wrong guess (verdict, mag call, a test where the trouble wasn't, a weight and balance that
  doesn't balance) costs $25, on the ticket as "Wrong guesses" and in the debrief's extras. A
  wrong move in a work scene, or an over-torque, costs only the try.
- Mail order arrives at once, 25% over shelf price. Odd jobs just pay.
- Badges record the rescue count they were earned at, not a day. Logbook entries carry no day.
- Saves: version 3; `_migrate` drops `day` and each job's `deadline` and `arrived`.
- The amounts are game balance, placeholders until logistics is designed (Thad).

## Acceptance

Self-test (`_no_days_ok`): every page of the wallet, logbook and badge board, map and career shows
no day figure; the fee shows per job; an old version-2 save loses its clock. A wrong mag call costs
the charge; a call left alone through an inspection is still there; a new call has no deadline;
pay after a delivery is less the fee.
