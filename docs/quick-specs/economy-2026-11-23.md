# An economy that teaches (P7)

**Status**: built, iteration 12
**Date**: 2026-11-23
**Code**: `main.ECONOMY`, `career_level`, `_pay_for` (used by `_spawn_job`)

## Why

Transactions should reflect the trade-offs the objectives teach: harder work is worth more, and
work you've outgrown is worth less (the economy pattern).

## What

A call's pay is its base (plane type, parts at cost, years sat, annual), plus:

- **A premium per step of its rating** (`call_difficulty`, 1 Routine to 5 Expert): $300 a step above
  Routine.
- **The rank's raise** (P19): Lead +10%, IA +20%.
- **A discount for outgrown work**: your career level rises one step every two rescues (1 to 5); a
  call rated below it pays 10% less per step below, never under 70%. Lesson calls are exempt.

Rounded to $50. The amounts are game balance in `ECONOMY`, not real-world values, and are to be
tuned in playtests. The map already shows each call's rating and what it would clear.

## Acceptance

Self-test: a harder call pays more than an easy one at the same base; the same easy call pays
less late in a career than early, never below the floor; a lesson call isn't discounted.
