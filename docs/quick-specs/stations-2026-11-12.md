# Quick Design Spec: Stations (work deeper without walking out)

**Type**: Addition
**System**: Work scenes (`scripts/work.gd`), the new `scripts/station.gd`, the work order in `main.gd`
**Date**: 2026-11-12

## Change Summary

Each spot on the plane opens up into a **station**: a grid of every place you can work there
(the engine has 27), shown with each place's own picture. Click one to work there. Esc or the Back
button returns to the station, not out to the walk-around. On Hard, the walk-around gives no clues:
you find what's wrong by opening the right place and looking.

## Rules

- **Entry.** The work order at a spot has "Open up the <spot>". "Work on it" on a found fault opens
  that place inside its station.
- **Labels** name the place or the job ("Spark plugs", "Pull a cylinder"), never the diagnosis.
  A scene opened before its fault is found is titled the same way.
- **One cowling.** The 16 engine scenes share the cowl and its six screws. Take it off once and it's
  off in every engine place. A job counts as finished without the cowl back on. The cowl can't
  close while another engine place is left apart ("Finish up at spark plugs first").
- **Sign-off.** The cowl must be on and screwed down. Easy and Medium say so on the work order.
  On Hard, flying off with it loose is a comeback (-$200).
- **Healthy places.** A place with nothing wrong, or with a fault you can't see by looking (a bad
  mag, a sticking valve) until a run-up or test finds it, opens with good parts and finished
  tasks. Nothing to buy, no readings. You can take it apart and put it back; it never finishes on
  its own.
- **Finding by looking.** Opening the place of a fault you can see (the walk-around kind) marks it found.
- **Hard has no clues.** Checking a spot says only "You look over the engine". The exceptions are
  faults with no place to open (a struck prop, kerosene in the sump), which are plain to see.
- **Tests** are places too. A test with a symptom behind it is the real one; otherwise everything
  reads normal and costs time.
- **Easy** marks each tile `!` (work waiting) or `OK`.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] The engine station lists 20+ places, with no socket hunt unless a socket was dropped.
- [x] A healthy place shows good parts, buys nothing and doesn't end.
- [x] The cowl taken off in one engine place is off in the next; a finished job leaves it off.
- [x] Opening the bird nest's place finds it.
- [x] All three difficulties pass the self-test; the station and Back button fit at 1280x720.
