# A job debrief (E5)

**Status**: built, iteration 5
**Date**: 2026-11-23
**Code**: `main.note_cost`, `job_debrief`, `_open_debrief`, `DEBRIEF_ADVICE`

## Why

Nothing helped the player reflect on a job: what it cost and what to do differently
(`docs/LEARNING_DESIGN.md` section D, "facilitate reflection", gap E5).

## What

- Each job records what cost extra as it happens (`job.lost`): a wrong mag call or verdict (time),
  a logbook entry sent back, a mail order (a day and 25%), a suspect replaced that wasn't the
  trouble (its part), a part broken by running it unready (its part). At sign-off: supplies used
  where nothing was wrong, comebacks, an open cowl.
- After delivery, a Debrief sheet (paper): days on site, pay, fuel, parts, rent and net; "Cost you
  extra", each item with its time or money; "Next time", one line for whatever cost the most, or
  "Clean job" when nothing did. "On to the next call" opens the map.
- The tenth plane goes straight to the ending; every debrief is kept in its logbook entry and shown
  there.
- It reports only: no money rule changes.

## Acceptance

- Self-test: after the delivery the Debrief sheet is open and lists the wrong mag call the test
  made, with advice; a job flown off with a missed fault shows the comeback and its advice.
- `--tutorial-shot` shoots the sheet.
