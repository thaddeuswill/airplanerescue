# Judge the mag drop (G3)

**Status**: built, iteration 3
**Date**: 2026-11-23
**Code**: `ferry.gd call_mag`, `mag_reason`; keys `call_good` (G) and `call_bad` (B)

## Why

The run-up read each mag's drop for the player and decided it for them, so the run-up taught
nothing (`docs/LEARNING_DESIGN.md`, gap G3).

## What

- After each mag's drop is read, the cockpit checklist asks for a call: good (G) or bad (B).
- Good is a drop above zero and no more than 75 rpm, running smooth (C-85, `JOBS_ATA.md`). Past 75
  (it runs rough) is bad; no drop at all is bad (the mag isn't grounding: a broken P-lead).
- A right call says why. A wrong call says why, costs time like a wrong verdict
  (`WRONG_CALL_DAYS`), and asks again. Never money.
- The run-up finishes only when both mags are called.
- The 75 rpm limit is on the card on Easy and Medium, not on Hard.
- Every call writes a play-log line: `mag call left: said good, drop 35, right`.

## Acceptance

- Self-test: a wrong call is explained, spends time and leaves the mag uncalled; the run-up doesn't
  finish with a mag uncalled; right calls finish it; the three reasons (none, too much, inside the
  limit) say Bad, Bad, Good.
