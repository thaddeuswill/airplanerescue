# Achievement badges (P8)

**Status**: built, iteration 9
**Date**: 2026-11-23
**Code**: `main.BADGES`, `earn_badge`, `badge_on_offer`; hooks in `work.gd` (To Spec), `ferry.gd`
(Reads the Drop, Clean Start, Straight and True), `main._call` (Detective), `_sign_logbook` (By the
Book), `_sign_off` (Sharp Eyes, Clean Job); icons `textures/hud/badge.svg`, `badge_none.svg`

## Why

Feedback on the competencies a player has shown, tied to the learning objectives, and something
to collect (the achievement badge pattern; serious fun).

## What

Eight badges, one per skill, each earned once, at the moment its standard is met:

| Badge | System | Standard |
|---|---|---|
| Sharp Eyes | Walk-around | Deliver a plane with all 8 spots checked and nothing coming back |
| To Spec | Repair | Finish a job with torqued fasteners and no over-torque |
| Reads the Drop | Run-up | Call both mags right on a run-up, with no wrong call |
| Detective | Troubleshooting | Follow a trail to its cause with no wrong call |
| By the Book | Paperwork | Sign the logbook with no entry sent back |
| Clean Start | Flight | Start the engine with no failed attempt |
| Straight and True | Flight | Take off with no ground loop |
| Clean Job | Career | Deliver a plane with nothing extra on the debrief |

- Earning one: a banner, a chime, a play-log line.
- The board, top of the logbook (L): each badge earned (with the day) or not yet, with its standard.
- While unearned, the task list and the cockpit card say "Can earn: ..." where it can be earned.
- Saved (`badges`); no money, time or rule changes.

## Acceptance

Self-test: after a run that met two standards and missed four, exactly those two are earned and
the four withheld; the logbook board is shot.
