# Achievement tracker (P13)

**Status**: built, iteration 10
**Date**: 2026-11-23
**Code**: `main._setup_tracker`, `_refresh_tracker`, `_tracker_pulse`

The eight badges as small icons, bottom centre of the screen, in process order (walk-around to
career), greyed until earned. Earning one colours it in with a pulse and shows its name above the
row for a few seconds (with the banner and chime). Hidden over sheets, jobs and the cockpit;
refreshed when a save loads. Clear of the next-step icon (top left), the squawk (top right), the
pockets (bottom left) and the save mark (bottom right).

Acceptance: self-test checks the right icon for each badge, the row shown in the world and hidden
over a sheet; a screenshot run shows it.
