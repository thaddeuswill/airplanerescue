# Better achievements: a proposal (B1)

**Status**: proposal, iteration 25, for Thad to choose from
**Date**: 2026-11-24

## What's wrong with the badges now

Thad: "those ones we have now are cheap". Each of the seven is earned once, for one clean moment,
and most come in the first job or two: start the engine without a failed attempt, take off
without a ground loop, call two mags right. Nothing asks you to keep it up, take a harder job, or
make a hard call.

## What a badge should be

- **Earned over a career, not a moment**: a streak or a count, so it says you're good at it, not
  that you got it right once.
- **Asks for something you'd otherwise avoid**: the hard call, the old plane, the tricky fault.
- **One per skill**, so the board still reads as "what kind of mechanic you are".
- **Measured from records the game keeps today**, so each can be tested: earned when met,
  withheld when not.

## The proposed set (eight)

| # | Badge | Standard | Replaces | Why it's worth earning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Nothing Gets Past You** | 5 deliveries in a row with all 8 spots checked and no comeback | Sharp Eyes | A thorough walk-around every time, not once |
| 2 | **Calibrated Hand** | 10 jobs in a row with every torqued fastener in the band, no over-torque | To Spec | Consistency at the bench |
| 3 | **Mag Whisperer** | 10 run-ups in a row with both mags called right, at least 2 of them bad mags | Reads the Drop | Calling a bad mag is the hard part, so it has to be in there |
| 4 | **Troubleshooter** | Follow 5 trails to the cause with no wrong verdict, at least one a look-alike (battery or starter, plugs or magneto) | Detective | The look-alike is where guessing fails |
| 5 | **Steady Hands** | 10 ferry flights in a row: started first try, no ground loop, no forced landing | Clean Start + Straight and True | One flying badge, and it takes a run of them |
| 6 | **Clean Sheet** | 5 jobs in a row with nothing extra on the ticket | Clean Job | Clean once is luck; five in a row is how you work |
| 7 | **Barn Find** | Deliver a plane that sat 10 years or more, with no comeback | (new) | The long-neglected plane is the game's hardest kind of call |
| 8 | **The Hard Call** | Ground a plane that has to be grounded (metal in the oil, jet fuel, a prop strike) instead of flying it | (new) | Saying no is part of the job, and the game should reward it |

Each "in a row" streak resets on the job that breaks it and is shown as progress on the board
("Clean Sheet: 3 of 5"), so a near miss reads as close rather than nothing.

## The rank ladder

IA needs 6 badges today (of 7 since L1). With harder badges, proposed: **Lead Mechanic** keeps
5 planes and 3 clean jobs; **IA** needs 8 planes and **4 of the 8 badges**, no comeback in the
last 5. (Game rules, not FAA requirements; marked for the playtest like the economy.)

## Saves

Careers keep what maps: Sharp Eyes, To Spec, Reads the Drop, Detective and Clean Job are not carried
over to the harder versions (they'd hand out a streak nobody ran); the board starts the new set
fresh, and the streak counters start at zero. A save-version bump drops the old keys.

## For Thad to choose

Keep, cut or change any of the eight; change a number (5 in a row, 10 years...); or add one. The
build then follows the chosen set, each standard tested in the self-test.
