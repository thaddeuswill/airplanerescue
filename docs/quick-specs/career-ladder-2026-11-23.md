# Job status: a career ladder (P19)

**Status**: built, iteration 11
**Date**: 2026-11-23
**Code**: `main.RANKS`, `LEAD_NEEDS`, `IA_NEEDS`, `earned_rank`, `rank_next_line`, `_check_promotion`; `rank` (saved)

## Why

Good on-the-job performance should bring tangible benefits the player can see coming (the job
status pattern), and the ladder should mirror a real A&P career.

## What

| Rank | Earned by (game rules) | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Apprentice | The three lessons | |
| A&P Mechanic | Finishing the apprenticeship | |
| Lead Mechanic | 5 planes delivered, 3 clean jobs (nothing extra on the debrief), no comeback in the last 3 | New calls pay 10% more |
| Inspection Authorization (IA) | 8 planes delivered, 6 badges, no comeback in the last 5 | New calls pay 20% more; annual inspections open |

- In real life an annual inspection needs an IA, so the game offers no annuals below it. The
  numbers that earn each rank are this game's rules, not FAA requirements (a real IA needs an A&P
  held three years and a test).
- Checked on each delivery; a promotion is a banner, a chime and a play-log line. A rank is never
  lost once earned.
- The logbook shows the rank, the next one and what it still needs, in figures.
- A career from before the ladder gets the rank its record earns when it loads.

## Acceptance

Self-test: a record that meets Lead is promoted; two clean jobs, or a comeback in the last three,
holds it back; eight planes and six badges earn IA; the same call pays 10% more at Lead; 150
calls spawned below IA include no annual.
