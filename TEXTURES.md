# Textures needed

Drop finished files into `textures/` with these exact names and I'll wire them in.

**Style for all of them:** low-poly look, so keep them simple and chunky. 128–256 px is plenty, PNG, **seamless/tileable** unless noted. Little fine detail; flat or lightly painted beats photo-real.

|---|---|---|

| `ground_snow.png` | Snow | Denver, Fargo… |
| `runway_asphalt.png` | Plain asphalt, no markings | Markings are separate geometry |

| `metal_rust.png` | Rusty, corroded metal | Broken parts before repair |
